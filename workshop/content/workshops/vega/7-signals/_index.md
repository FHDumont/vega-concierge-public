Evaluators catch the problems you knew to measure. Agentic systems also fail in ways you didn't anticipate — planning loops, routing failures, recurring tool errors. **Signals** find those *unknown unknowns*.

{{% notice title="Persona" style="orange" icon="user" %}}
As Vega Retail Co.'s **AI engineer**, you can't write an evaluator for every failure mode. Signals tell you proactively: *here's a pattern going wrong, and here's what to do next.*
{{% /notice %}}

> [!splunk] **Signals** automatically surface recurring failure patterns from production traces with actionable context — turning weeks of post-incident analysis into minutes of targeted remediation.

{{% notice title="What Signals detect" style="info" %}}
Planning loops, tool errors, hallucinations, routing failures — and emerging patterns you never thought to define.
{{% /notice %}}
