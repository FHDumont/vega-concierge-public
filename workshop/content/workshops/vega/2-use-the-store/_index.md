Before you break anything on purpose, experience Vega as a shopper. The store is real — 28 SKUs, USD, American English — and every AI touchpoint becomes a Splunk Agent Observability trace once the key is set.

> [!splunk] **The customer view.**
> Shoppers see a normal e-commerce site. Wrong answers, tool failures, and policy inventions do not raise HTTP errors — they surface as subtle UX bugs. That is why classic dashboards stay green while trust erodes.

{{% notice title="Where to work" style="info" %}}
Storefront at **`http://<host>:3000`**. Open the sidebar (☰) for Store / Account / Admin (after owner login).
{{% /notice %}}

Continue through browse, concierge, checkout, and account — the flows you'll later stress with workshop use cases.
