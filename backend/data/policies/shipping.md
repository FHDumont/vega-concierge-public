# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery time

Standard shipping is estimated at **2 business days** for in-stock items. The estimate starts when payment is confirmed, not when the order is placed. Items marked out of stock do not ship until they are restocked.

## Shipping cost

Orders of **$200 or more** qualify for **free standard shipping**. Orders below $200 are charged **$9.99** for standard shipping at checkout. Vega does not offer paid express shipping — there is a single shipping speed for all orders.

## Where we ship

Vega ships to **US domestic** addresses only (all 50 states plus Washington, D.C.). We do not ship to PO boxes that cannot receive carrier delivery, APO/FPO, or international addresses. We cannot forward orders to a freight forwarder.

## Tracking your order

Every order moves through the same states: paid, then shipped, then delivered. You can see the current state and its history on the order page in your account. A notification email goes out when the order is confirmed and again when it ships, including a tracking link when available.

## Changing the delivery address

The address can be changed only while the order is still awaiting shipment. Once the order is marked as shipped, the address is locked with the carrier and cannot be changed. Contact support if the carrier has not yet picked up the package.

## Delivery issues

If tracking shows delivered but you did not receive the package, contact us within 48 hours with your order number. If an item arrives damaged, report it within {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days — we will arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra shipping cost.
