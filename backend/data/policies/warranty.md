# Warranty

## Coverage period

Every product sold by Vega carries a **12-month manufacturer warranty** against defects, counted from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage.

## What is covered

Components that fail under normal use are covered: batteries that no longer hold a charge, buttons or switches that stop responding, speakers or microphones that fail, and charging ports that stop working. Repair or replacement is at Vega's discretion; if the item is no longer available, we refund it.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage beyond the product's stated water resistance rating, cosmetic wear, and damage caused by unauthorized repair are not covered. Consumable parts such as ear tips and cushions are not covered.

## Warranty versus return

The warranty is separate from the return window. A return is a {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}}-day no-reason refund. A warranty claim is a 12-month defect repair or replacement, and it is not automatically a refund. After the {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}}-day return window closes, a defective item is handled as a warranty claim.

## Starting a claim

Warranty claims are opened through customer support with the order number. Proof of purchase is the order record itself — you do not need to keep the packaging. Describe the defect and we will provide a prepaid return label or troubleshooting steps within two business days.
