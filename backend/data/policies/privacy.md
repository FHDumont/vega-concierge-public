# Privacy Policy

## What we collect

Vega collects only what is needed to run the store: your name, email, shipping address, order history, and payment status. We do not collect government IDs, precise location beyond the address you provide, or social media profiles.

## How we use your data

Order data is used to fulfill purchases, send transactional emails (confirmation, shipping, returns), and compute loyalty tier. We do not sell personal information to third parties. Aggregated sales metrics may be used internally for inventory and workshop demos.

## Cookies and local storage

The storefront uses browser local storage for your session token, shopping cart, and anonymous shopper session ID used by the AI concierge. These identifiers are not shared across browsers or devices.

## AI concierge

When you use product Q&A, store chat, or the Vega AI concierge, your questions and relevant order or product context are sent to configured LLM providers to generate a response. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat that you would not email to support. Conversation content may appear in observability tools when workshop tracing is enabled.

## Data retention

Account and order records are kept for the life of your account plus a reasonable period for tax and chargeback compliance. You may request account deletion by contacting support; anonymized order aggregates may remain in admin reports.

## Contact

For privacy questions, email support@vega.demo or use the contact form in your account area.
