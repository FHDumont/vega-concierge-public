# Payment

## Accepted methods

Vega accepts **credit card** and **Pix** (demo). All prices are in **US dollars (USD)** and already include applicable sales tax where required.

## When you are charged

The charge is authorized when you place the order and captured once the order is confirmed. If the order fails at any point before confirmation, **no charge is made** — an authorization that was never captured drops off your statement on its own.

## Security review

Some orders are held for a brief automated security review before being confirmed. A held order is not charged while it is under review. This is a routine precaution and does not mean the card was declined; you can retry the order or contact support.

## Declined payments

A declined card is usually a limit or a data mismatch on the bank's side. Vega never receives the reason for the decline, so support cannot tell you which check failed — contact your bank, or pay with Pix instead.

## Installments

Orders can be paid in a single installment only. Vega does not offer installment plans or buy-now-pay-later at checkout.

## Currency and pricing

All catalog prices, checkout totals, and refunds are denominated in USD. Your bank may apply its own conversion rate if your card is issued outside the United States.
